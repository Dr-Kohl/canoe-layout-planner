Canoe Layout Planner

Plan the cuts before you build.

Your team receives 55 feet of 41-inch-wide waxed paper board. Reserve the skin first, then see how much remains for the 10-inch rolled frame pieces.

1. Set the stock plan

Continuous skin allowance
Each strip includes the canoe length, both side heights, and 6 in for a 3 in wrap at each end. Add additional allowance for canoe front end.
Tube-direction rule
Cut each tube strip along the long direction of the paperboard. At 10 in wide, this uses 4 full lanes with 1 in left for trim. Tubes rolled in the same direction of its curvature tend to be weaker with more creases.

2. Add frame parts

Enter the length of each piece before it is rolled.
