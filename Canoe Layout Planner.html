Canoe Layout Planner

Plan the cuts before you build.

Your team receives 55 feet of 41-inch-wide waxed paper board. Reserve the skin first, then see how much remains for the 10-inch rolled frame pieces.

1. Set the stock plan

Continuous skin allowance
Each strip includes the canoe length, both side heights, and 6 in for a 3 in wrap at each end.
Tube-direction rule
Cut each tube strip along the long direction of the paperboard. At 10 in wide, this uses 4 full lanes with 1 in left for trim. Do not turn short pieces across the sheet; that makes weaker, creased tubes.

2. Add frame parts

Enter the length of each piece before it is rolled.
